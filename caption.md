We usually only picture the tray that lands on a school desk at lunch, the work behind it happens long before anyone sits down, rice sacks, wash basins, a line of steel ompreng waiting to be filled

I built a small simulation of one day inside an MBG kitchen to get a feel for that rhythm, in my version the day starts at three in the morning and ends when the last van pulls back in

Having studied environmental health, what stays with me is how much of a good meal is really timing and hygiene, clean hands, separate boards, food that moves from the wok to the classroom without waiting around

It is only a toy model with sample numbers, but watching it run made me look at every tray a little differently
